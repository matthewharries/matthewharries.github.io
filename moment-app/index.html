Questions, bug reports or ideas? Email matthewharriesdev@gmail.com and I'll get back to you.

Syncing between devices

Moment syncs through your iCloud account. For your moments to show up on every device, sign in to the same Apple Account on each one and make sure iCloud is turned on for Moment (Settings › your name › iCloud). New moments usually appear within a minute or so.

Backing up your journal

In Moment's Settings, under Data, you can export everything as a PDF to print or keep, or as a JSON file with a complete copy of your journal.

Daily reminder

Turn on the daily reminder in Settings and pick a time. If it doesn't arrive, check that notifications are allowed for Moment in your device's Settings.

Privacy

Moment doesn't collect any data. Your journal stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. Read the full privacy policy.
